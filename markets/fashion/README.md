# Gulf fashion stores

Online stores selling adult clothing (women's, men's or unisex) per market. Each workbook has `Fashion Stores`, `Excluded` and `Coverage & Method` sheets; the CSV mirrors `Fashion Stores`.

- **Saudi Arabia** (`Saudi_Arabia_Fashion_Stores.xlsx` / `.csv`): 974 stores. Columns: Store Name, Website, Type, Gender, Platform, Size Tier, Verification Status, Evidence URL. Method: live scan of the Saudi master directory and unverified candidates (homepage nav, Shopify/Woo/Salla APIs, sitemaps, Playwright for JS menus) plus App Store, Common Crawl and seed-list discovery; Arabic and English clothing keywords. Known gaps: bot-walled or JS-only storefronts (esp. small Zid stores) are under-covered, majors are partly accepted manually, Gender/Type/Size Tier are heuristic, scanned from a US VM.
- **UAE** (`UAE_Fashion_Stores.xlsx` / `.csv`): 357 stores. Columns: Store Name, Website, Type, Gender, Platform, Size Tier, Verification Status, Evidence URL. Method: live classification of verified directory stores and promoted unverified candidates (nav keywords, Shopify/Woo data), plus a recall pass (Playwright, Common Crawl `*.ae`, UAE-locale probes of global brands, App Store). Known gaps: nav-keyword signals are thin on JS-rendered sites, bot-walled majors are manually accepted, Gender/Type/Size Tier are heuristic.
- **Qatar** (`Qatar_Fashion_Stores.xlsx` / `.csv`): 117 stores. Columns: Store Name, Website, Type, Gender, Platform, Size Tier (best-effort), Verification Status, Evidence URL. Method: live fetch of directory and unverified candidates (nav and meta, Shopify/Woo APIs, EN+AR keywords), Qatar-storefront probes of about 220 GCC and global brands, and a Common Crawl scan of the `.qa` TLD. Known gaps: bot-walled majors rely on secondary evidence or are flagged unverified, Gender is partly inferred for fetch-failed sites, no bulk Common Crawl or Wayback pass.
- **Kuwait** (`Kuwait_Fashion_Stores.xlsx` / `.csv`): 116 stores. Columns: Store Name, Website, Type, Gender, Platform, Size Tier (best-effort), Verification Status, Evidence URL, Classification Signal, Discovery Source. Method: live classification of the Kuwait directory plus App Store, brand-pattern and Kuwait-locale discovery, then a second pass with unverified-row review and QA sampling. Known gaps: Common Crawl, Wayback and search engines were unreachable, Instagram-only brands and bot-walled Kuwait locales are under-covered, 4 rows remain unverified.

Cleanup applied to all four files: duplicate, non-market, marketplace, kids-only, accessories/footwear-only and non-store rows were moved to `Excluded` (source "Deliverable cleanup (fashion-stores package)") and Website values were normalised to clean storefront URLs. The by-source, by-channel and QA breakdowns in `Coverage & Method` are as at the original run.
